## Candidate datasets and expected output variables

**ML task:** from a customer's description of a home problem, predict (1) the service group / trade and (2) a price range.

| # | Dataset | Source | License | Size | Expected-output variable(s) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Urban Company India Services Data | Kaggle (hardikmirani) | GNU Free Documentation License v1.3 (as listed on Kaggle) | 8,530 rows x 6 columns | `service` (trade, for the classifier); `subservice_charge` (price, for the regressor) | **Selected** |
| 2 | Self-employed pricing rates 2026 (WhatToCharge) | Hugging Face | CC BY 4.0 | 226 rows x 9 columns | `typical_usd`, `low_usd`, `high_usd` (price); `category` / `trade` | Not selected |
| 3 | PropertyPilot tickets | Hugging Face (0tizm0) | MIT, with the card narrowing use to research and education | Unclear: 10,500 or 24.5k rows, 20 columns | `category` (10 classes, e.g. Plumbing, Electrical, Appliances); `cost_usd` | Not selected (possible supplement) |
| 4 | Yelp Open Dataset | Yelp | Terms not confirmed; the Yelp evaluation licence I found prohibits using content to train ML models | Large | `stars` (provider rating) | Not selected |

### Why Urban Company was chosen
- It is the only candidate with **service names and real prices for the trades in the app** (plumbing, electrical, AC and microwave repair).
- Its structure fits the planned outputs: a trade/service label for the classifier and a numeric charge for the price model.
- It is complete (no missing values) and covers 43 cities.

### Why the others were not
- **WhatToCharge:** US prices in USD, units vary (hour, job, square foot), and the card does not say where the ranges come from or how they were produced. Useful only as a rough cross-check of price ranges.
- **PropertyPilot:** synthetic text generated by a language model, with quality problems the card itself reveals (leaked prompt text, mismatched notes, inconsistent row counts). Not reliable as primary data, but its English tenant messages could supplement classifier training after cleaning.
- **Yelp:** provider ratings and reviews, which are outside the chosen scope, and the terms I could read restrict ML use, so it should not be used without confirming the applicable licence.

### Licence note
Kaggle lists the dataset under the GNU Free Documentation License v1.3. That licence was written for documentation, not data, so it is an unusual fit. The prices were collected from Urban Company's public pages, so rights held by Urban Company may not be covered by the Kaggle licence. The dataset is used here for coursework only, and the source and licence are credited.

### Limitations common to all candidates
- None contains Kenyan prices or Swahili text. The search was limited and did not find an open Kenyan home-services price dataset.
- Prices in the selected dataset are in Indian rupees and need calibration with local Nairobi quotes.
